# StockClub

Your community. Your ticker. Your treasury.

Stock clubs on Base. The board starts empty. Create a ticker, join it, post, and keep a simulated treasury. Live quotes come from Yahoo Finance. Coinbase tokenized-stock NAV is shown when a club matches NVDA, TSLA, or AAPL. No contracts are deployed. A vote records intent and does not place a trade.

## Try it

- Connect a demo Base wallet. Nothing is signed and no funds move.
- Create a club and open it at `/club/yourticker`.
- Post, contribute simulated USDC, and check the leaderboard.

## Stack

Vite app. Wallet, clubs, and treasury stay in the browser for this phase.
